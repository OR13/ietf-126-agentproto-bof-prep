# Official IETF slide assets — DO NOT MODIFY

These files are **official IETF artifacts**, committed **verbatim**. They must
not be edited, reformatted, or summarized.

## IESG rule for the Note Well (quoted)

> "Preserve the text as is, preserve the links and not add anything to it. Do
> not add the Note Really Well slide or create any other slides about policies."

Chairs must also not interpret or get into the substance of the policies, and
must not dismiss the importance of any item on the Note Well.
