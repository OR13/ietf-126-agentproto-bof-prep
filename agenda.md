## Consensus questions

The chairs will take a sense of the room on the following. Final consensus is
confirmed on the mailing list.

1. Is the problem statement clear, well-scoped, and worth solving?
2. Is the IETF the right venue for this work?
3. Should this be a single working group (as chartered) rather than several?
4. Given the charter as posted and any revisions discussed at the session
   (to be finalized on the list), should a working group be formed?
5. (And the converse: how many feel a working group should not be formed?)
6. Who is willing to review documents and contribute on the mailing list?
7. Who is willing to serve as a document editor?
